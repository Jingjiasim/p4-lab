C4 — Tunnel loop after hop-limit implementation

Configuration:
s2 tunnel destination 3 redirected to port 1 (towards s1).

Command:
sh ./p4cli s2 "table_modify MyIngress.myTunnel_exact MyIngress.myTunnel_forward 2 1"

Packet sent:
h1 python3 send.py 10.19.0.3 "loop after" --tunnel 3 --hops 4

Initial tunnel hop_limit: 4

First measurement:
logs/s1.txt:1434
logs/s2.txt:1433

After 5 seconds:
logs/s1.txt:1434
logs/s2.txt:1433

Change in packet counts:
s1: 0
s2: 0

Observation:
The packet counts remained unchanged after 5 seconds.
This is consistent with the hop-limit mechanism terminating
the forwarding loop rather than allowing indefinite circulation.

Comparison:
Before hop limit: packet processing continued between s1 and s2.
After hop limit: packet processing stopped.
